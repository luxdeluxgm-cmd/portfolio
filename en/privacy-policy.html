Privacy Policy

Data controller and contact

The controller of your personal data is Jan Tokarzewski. Write to me at kontakt@jantokarzewski.pl or use the contact form on the website.

What data I collect and why

I only collect what you provide to me yourself: your name (or company name), email address, and the content of the message from the form, as well as data contained in an email or provided over the phone. I use them exclusively to read your inquiry and respond to it. Providing data is voluntary, but without it, I will not be able to answer you.

Legal basis

I process data to respond to your inquiry and possibly establish terms of cooperation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and within the framework of my legitimate interest, which is handling correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who else has access to the data

The form is handled by the Formspree service (Formspree, Inc., USA). After clicking the send button, your message goes to their servers, and from there to my email inbox. Formspree, like any internet service, also sees basic technical connection data, e.g., IP address.

The website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). During each visit, it processes the IP address and basic technical data to display and secure the website. Cloudflare also redirects emails sent to addresses in my domain to my inbox, which is managed by an email service provider.

Data transferred to the USA is protected by approved mechanisms: standard contractual clauses (Formspree, Cloudflare) and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Cloudflare). If you write to me through the Useme platform, your data is also processed by Useme, on their own terms. I do not sell data and do not share it with anyone other than the mentioned entities.

How long I keep the data

I delete messages and correspondence 12 months after the last contact. If we conclude a contract, I may keep some data longer when required by law (e.g., tax or accounting regulations). Formspree stores copies of form submissions in its panel for a limited time, depending on the service plan.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or transfer, as well as object to its processing. All you have to do is write to the address provided above. I do not make automated decisions and do not profile visitors. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies or any analytical tools (such as Google Analytics). Cloudflare, which hosts the website, may save a technical cookie protecting it against bots.

Demo websites

The sample websites at demo.jantokarzewski.pl are showcase projects with fictional data. Their forms do not send anything, and the shopping cart in the demo store is saved only in your browser.
